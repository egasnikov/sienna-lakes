The asset

A golf-front Serena in original condition, priced at 3,132 AED/sqft on existing BUA

Asking 23.8M for a vacant 5-bed Serena on a 9,992 sqft plot with direct golf and lake frontage. Structure, marble floors and layout are sound; the pool, facade, kitchen, bathrooms and landscaping date from handover. The plan: buy at 23.0M, invest 10M in a full refit plus a 1,400 sqft extension to 9,000 sqft BUA over 10 months, reposition and sell within 2 months at 45M, with the comp set supporting 45 to 50M.
